PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date: November 9, 2026 · The short version: we barely collect anything.

Who we are

TUG'S is a sports bar & restaurant located at 1200 Ocean Drive, Miami Beach, FL 33139, operated by Core Restaurants Miami Beach ("TUG'S", "we", "us"). This policy explains what information is collected when you visit this website and how it is used. Questions? Email us at management@tugsmiamibeach.com.

What we collect

This website is a simple informational site. It has no user accounts, no forms, and it does not ask you for personal information. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

Like almost every website, some technical data is processed automatically when you visit:

  • Server logs. Our site is hosted on GitHub Pages (operated by GitHub, Inc.). GitHub may log standard technical data such as your IP address for security and operational purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Fonts. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser requests those font files from Google's servers, which involves transmitting your IP address to Google. See the Google Privacy Policy.

We do not have access to, and do not compile, any of this technical data ourselves.

If you contact us

If you email or call us — for example about a job, an event, or feedback — we will use the information you share only to respond to you. Job application materials are used only for hiring purposes.

Cookies

This site does not set cookies of its own. For details — and what will change if we ever add analytics — see our Cookie Policy.

Links to other sites

Our site links to third-party services such as Instagram. Those services have their own privacy practices, which we don't control. Check their policies before sharing information with them.

Online ordering & reservations

If and when we offer online ordering or reservations, they will be handled by a third-party provider. That provider will process your order, contact and payment details under its own privacy policy, and we will update this page to name them.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices & rights

Since we don't build profiles or maintain marketing databases from this website, there's usually nothing for us to delete — but if you've emailed us and want that correspondence removed, just ask and we'll take care of it. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy laws; contact us and we'll do our best to honor them.

Changes to this policy

If we change how this website handles information — for example, by adding analytics, a newsletter, or online ordering — we will update this policy and change the effective date at the top of this page.

That's it. Now go look at the menu.
