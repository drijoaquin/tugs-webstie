ABOUT TUG'S

The bulldog runs the place. We just work here.

TUG'S is a new high-energy bar & restaurant opening at 1200 Ocean Drive, in the heart of South Beach. Cold beer, smash burgers, fried everything, frozen drinks, sports and a lot of fun — an unpretentious neighborhood spot built around Tug, our bulldog.

No reservations, no attitude. High volume all day and late into the night, with every game on and the frozen machines spinning. Come for the Dog Park — our build-your-own hot dog program — stay for the beer towers.

"He'd order the double." — Tug
